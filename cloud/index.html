Cybersecurity Workforce Research — Platform Opportunity Disparity Analysis

Analyzed over 65,000 job postings using the NICE Framework taxonomy to classify cybersecurity roles across Missouri and nationally.

Conducted Python-based analysis (pandas, matplotlib, seaborn) of Handshake vs. CyberSeek datasets, uncovering an 87.5× disparity in cybersecurity opportunity visibility across platforms.

Findings have significant implications for how students discover and access cybersecurity careers — revealing a critical gap between where opportunities exist and where students are looking.
